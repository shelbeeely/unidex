RoomiePet

The ChoreGotchi Ecosystem

A cooperative household Tamagotchi for roommates, on the Xteink X4. Everyone has their own Pet on their own X4; the Pets share a household, and keeping it up is the game. Do your chores and your Pet is proud, levels up and sometimes finds a gift, like loot from a game. Let the bins slide and it sulks.

Pets

A few of the looks a Pet can have, some wearing their owner's pronoun pin. Tap one to try it on below.

Dress up

Seven layers, drawn exactly as the X4 draws them (clothes under the face, hair under hats), plus a pin with your pronouns or your pride flag.

Pride flags

Pick a flag for your Pet: it shows beside its name, on its name tag when it meets other Pets and in their friends lists, on your household's Roommates, and on its pin if you like. The X4's screen is black and white, so it draws each colour as a texture (below each flag), and no two stripes next to each other look the same.

Pronouns

Each roommate can set their pronouns (he/him, she/her, they/them, he/they, she/they, any, it/its, xe/xem, ze/zir, fae/faer and more on the X4, or anything up to 12 characters from the web app). They show:

  • beside your Pet's name on its screen, "Pip (she/her)";
  • on its pronoun pin, if it wears one: a badge on its chest, wherever your Pet is drawn big enough to read it;
  • on its name tag when it meets other Pets, and on their friends lists;
  • on your household's Roommates, on every roommate's X4 and in the web app.

The wardrobe

Every part, worn by the plain blob. Everyday pieces are starters every Pet has, so nobody has to earn being themselves. The showy ones are gifts: nothing is bought or spent, they drop after chores.

How gifts drop

Every chore your Pet does has a chance of a gift, then a roll for how rare it is, then a piece it hasn't found yet (never a duplicate). How the chore went sways the odds:

The chance of a gift20%, plus
Done by its due day+10%
Covering a roommate's turn+15%
Rescuing a late chore+10%
Your first chore today+10%
Each day in a row with a chore (up to 5)+3%
Four chores without a giftthe next one always brings one

Then the rarity: 3% legendary, 22% rare, the rest uncommon, with better odds of rare and legendary for helping out, rescues and streaks. Chores still give EXP (10, or 15 on time), but only for your Pet's level: it's never spent.

What it does

Chores on a rota

Whose turn it is, when it's due and what's late. Mark it done on the X4 or your phone, and the turn moves on. Your Pet cheers when a roommate finishes theirs.

Shopping, reminders, messages

A shared list, bin day and rent reminders, and messages your Pet passes on to theirs ("Out of milk!").

Money and Cash App

Who owes whom, split fairly and settled in the fewest payments. The X4 shows a QR code to pay whoever's owed, with the amount, or a Cash App request you pasted. Share it by text, email or the share sheet.

No server, no account

Hold two X4s near each other and they swap the household over their own radio. Every device ends up the same, whatever order the changes arrive in.

A web app for all of it

On your phone over the X4's own WiFi (House, Manage on phone), or here on a computer over USB: the X4 manager. Its household rules are the X4's own C++, compiled to WebAssembly.

Built on FreeInk

The X4's screen, buttons, battery, SD card and sleep run through the FreeInk SDK, the same base as CrossPoint Reader.
