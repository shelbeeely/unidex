# RoomiePet's catalog: things to hold and clothes to wear. One line each, "Name, sprite[, kind]". Names are up to 12
# characters; the order is the order they're offered in. The kind is where it goes: hand (the default: held), hat,
# accessory (at the neck), hair or outfit; catalog clothes are worn instead of the wardrobe's part for that layer.
# A sprite is either
#   kenney:<number>   a tile of Kenney's 1-Bit Pack (kenney-1-bit-pack/sheet.png, CC0: numbered from 0, left to right,
#                     then row by row, 49 to a row; the web app's Items page shows every one with its number), or
#   <file>.png        a picture in sprites/:
#                       16 x 16: an icon (anything not transparent or white is ink), held, or worn on the head or neck
#                       32 x 32: a piece of the Pet's own grid, drawn to fit it (black ink, white paper, transparent
#                       see-through), for any kind of clothes; rows left empty top and bottom aren't stored
# After changing this file: python3 tools/items/build.py (it writes src/apps/pet/items.h and site/x4/items.js).
# An X4 can also take new things to hold from its web app, without new firmware, and passes them on to the X4s it
# meets (pet_logic.h's items, docs/ITEMS.md).
